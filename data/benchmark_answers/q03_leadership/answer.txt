During my final year project, our team of five was struggling to meet our sprint deadline because two members were unclear about their tasks. I took the initiative to organize a two-hour planning session where I broke down the backlog into clear user stories and assigned them based on each person's strengths. I then set up daily fifteen-minute stand-ups to track progress and unblock issues. As a result, we delivered the project on time and received the highest grade in our batch. This taught me that leadership is about enabling others, not just directing them.
